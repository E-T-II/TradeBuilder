# Preface

> **Status:** outline

Why this strategy exists, and why it is written as rules rather than intuition.

## Must cover

- [ ] The problem: most losing trades are not bad analysis, they are unmanaged
      risk and improvised sizing.
- [ ] The premise: if the trade is scored before it is taken, the decision to
      take it is already made when price arrives. No deciding in the moment.
- [ ] Why *engineered*: every number in a trade — stop, entry, target, size — is
      derived from inputs the trader can read off a chart, not chosen by feel.
- [ ] The one-line summary of the method: score the setup out of 10, and only
      if it qualifies, build the order.
- [ ] "Deviation is not in the program." What that sentence is protecting the
      reader from.

## Notes

Keep this short. The reader's first real chapter is the vocabulary; the preface
only has to earn their attention and set the tone that the rules are hard.

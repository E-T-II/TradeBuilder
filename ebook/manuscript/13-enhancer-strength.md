# Odds enhancer — Strength

> **Status:** outline

**The question: how did price leave the zone?**

## Scoring

| Score | Condition |
|---|---|
| **2** | Move out **and** breakout |
| **1** | Move out **only**, or breakout **only** |
| **0** | Neither |

## Must cover

- [ ] Define **move out**: the departure from the zone, and how far/fast it must
      be to count. State the measurable test.
- [ ] Define **breakout**: what structure price has to break for this to count.
- [ ] Charts for all three scores, for both a demand zone and a supply zone.
- [ ] Why departure strength is evidence of imbalance — an order that big left
      unfilled orders behind.
- [ ] The judgement call at the boundary, and how to break the tie conservatively.

## Notes

The app illustrates this with a reference graphic and asks the reader to pick
Best / Good / Poor — `src/components/strength-reference.tsx`. The captions there
are shorthand for the definitions this chapter owes the reader:

> "The stronger the Move out from the zone, with Breakout, the more likely that
> supply and demand are out of balance."

That sentence is the conclusion. This chapter has to supply the reasoning and
the measurable test behind it.

# Quick reference card

> **Status:** outline — intended to be printable, one or two pages

Everything the reader needs at the chart once they know the method. No
explanation, only the rules and the arithmetic.

## Must cover

### Before the chart

- [ ] Risk tolerance ≤ 2% · Target buffer 75–80% · Stop buffer 2% or 10% of ATR

### The six steps

- [ ] 1. Curve on HTF → thirds: wholesale / equilibrium / retail
- [ ] 2. Trend on ITF → up / sideways / down
- [ ] 3. Zones on LTF → supply distal, supply proximal, demand proximal, demand distal
- [ ] 4. Decision Matrix → long / short / no trade
- [ ] 5. Score → six enhancers out of 10
- [ ] 6. S.E.T.S. → stop, entry, target, size → place the order

### Scoring at a glance

- [ ] Strength 2/1/0 · Time 1/0.5/0 · Freshness 2/1/0
- [ ] Trend 2/1/0 · Curve 1/0.5/0 · Profit zone 2/1/0
- [ ] 8.5+ proximal · 7–8 confirmation · under 7 no trade

### The formulas

- [ ] Stop buffer = ATR × (2% or 10%), round **up**
- [ ] Stop = distal ∓ stop buffer
- [ ] Target = entry proximal ± (profit zone × target buffer %)
- [ ] Trade risk per share = entry − stop
- [ ] Max account risk = balance × 2%
- [ ] Shares = max account risk ÷ risk per share, round **down**
- [ ] Capital requirement = shares × entry, must be ≤ 50% of balance
- [ ] Reward:risk = (target − entry) ÷ risk per share, must be ≥ 3:1

### The hard stops

- [ ] Reward:risk below 3:1 → no trade
- [ ] Total open risk above 6% → no trade
- [ ] Not scored → not taken
- [ ] Angry, excited or cocky → not trading

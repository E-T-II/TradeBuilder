> **Status:** outline

This book is educational material, not financial advice. Nothing here is a
recommendation to buy or sell any security.

## Must cover

- [ ] Education only; no advice, no solicitation, no performance promise.
- [ ] Trading involves risk of loss, including total loss of capital.
- [ ] The reader is responsible for their own trades and their own broker.
- [ ] Worked examples use invented prices; they are arithmetic demonstrations,

- [ ] Any software built from this book (the Trade Builder) is a calculator. It

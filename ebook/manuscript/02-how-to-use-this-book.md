# How to use this book

> **Status:** outline

## Must cover

- [ ] The book follows the order you actually work in: defaults first, then the
      six steps, then the risk rules that can veto everything.
- [ ] Read it once end to end before trading a single setup from it. The steps
      interlock — the Decision Matrix in step 3 depends on the curve from step 1
      and the trend from step 2.
- [ ] The running example used throughout: a $600 account, 2% risk tolerance,
      NVDA with a daily ATR of 5.93, a curve from 100 to 130, a demand zone at
      106–108 and a supply zone at 124–126.
- [ ] Where a second ATR (4.00) is worked against the same zones, it is a
      deliberate cross-check: two inputs through one rule, so an error in the
      rule cannot hide behind a single tidy example.
- [ ] How chapters are structured: the rule, the arithmetic, the chart, the
      failure mode.
- [ ] The companion calculator (Trade Builder) automates the arithmetic, but the
      reader must still supply the three judged scores — strength, time and
      freshness — by reading the chart themselves.
- [ ] What this book does **not** cover: choosing what to trade, fundamentals,
      taxes, broker selection.

## Notes

State plainly that the calculator is downstream of the book. A reader who
understands the book can rebuild the calculator; a reader who only has the
calculator cannot rebuild the book.

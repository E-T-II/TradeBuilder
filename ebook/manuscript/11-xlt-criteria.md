# XLT Criteria

> **Status:** drafted

The Decision Matrix has four conditional cells — the XLT cells (c, f, m and
p). Each one trades from a hostile corner of the matrix, so the matrix only
approves it when the chart earns the extra room. The four cells pair up into
two scenarios, each with a short setup and its mirrored long:

- **Scenario 1 — counter-trend reversal.** Short supply high on the curve in
  an ITF uptrend (cell c), or buy demand low on the curve in an ITF downtrend
  (cell p). You are anticipating the ITF trend ending at the curve extreme.
  Minimum **3:1** profit zone; **confirmation entry only**.
- **Scenario 2 — aggressive trend continuation.** Short supply low on the
  curve in an ITF downtrend (cell m), or buy demand high on the curve in an
  ITF uptrend (cell f). You are going *with* the trend, but at the curve
  extreme, potentially against higher-timeframe opposing order flow. Minimum
  **5:1** profit zone.

The two scenarios sit opposite each other in the matrix. The Scenario 1 short
at retail supply faces the Scenario 2 long buying retail demand in the same
uptrend; the Scenario 1 long at wholesale demand faces the Scenario 2 short
selling wholesale supply in the same downtrend. One trader expects the trend
to continue, the other expects it to reverse — the XLT criteria decide which
side of that argument the chart actually supports.

XLT never changes the entry type. An XLT cell adds a minimum profit-zone
score and the chart checks below; the odds-enhancer scorecard still decides
the order at face value, exactly as it does for every other setup:

- **8.5 – 10** → proximal entry (limit order at the proximal line).
- **7 – 8** → confirmation entry (10¢ before the proximal line).
- **Below 7** → no trade.

The app calculates the curve, trend, profit-zone score, and risk math. For
every XLT cell, the scorecard must show strength **2**, freshness **2**, time
at least **0.5**, and a total of at least **7**. The profit-zone enhancer must
meet the scenario's 3:1 or 5:1 threshold. The Trade Builder reports unmet
entry-score requirements and does not ask the trader to check them off. The
target zone's score is for comparison only: the app does not use it to qualify
the target or block a setup. The trader judges target-zone quality and the
intervening structures.

The manual checklist is reserved for chart facts the scorecard cannot assess:
formation type, the 5-candle disqualification, price arrival, intervening
structures, and the relevant higher-timeframe check. Those checks must still
be verified before committing capital.

## Structural quality and imbalance — both scenarios

Every XLT entry zone must be a clean institutional formation: for a short, a
drop-base-drop or rally-base-drop; for a long, the mirror rally-base-rally or
drop-base-rally. Confirm each of the following on the low timeframe:

- **Strength.** The move away from the base origin must be rapid and
  explosive — at least **2:1 with breakout** (3:1 is optimal), per the
  strength odds enhancer. That is full marks: *move out AND breakout*. A
  move-out only or a breakout only **disqualifies** the setup. The explosive
  departure is the evidence that significant institutional orders were left
  behind waiting to be filled.
- **Time.** The base must be clean and tight: no more than **3 to 4 tightly
  packed candles**, and 1 to 2 is optimal. Score time as you normally would
  (1 – 3 candles → 1 point, 4 – 6 candles → 0.5), but a base of **5 or more
  candles disqualifies** the setup even though the enhancer would still award
  half a point. The less time price spent at the zone, the greater the chance
  of unfilled orders remaining there.
- **Freshness.** The zone must be **completely fresh and untested** — full
  marks on the freshness enhancer. A zone that has been retested invalidates
  the setup, because the retest may already have dissolved the unfilled
  orders.
- **Price arrival.** Price must return to the zone in a strong impulsion
  phase: long-legged candles driving back into the zone before the reversal.
  Slow, grinding, or choppy consolidated structures on the way back may
  invalidate the setup — they suggest structural institutional
  redistribution rather than a simple retest.

## Line placement and risk parameters — both scenarios

- **Proximal line.** At the nearest candle *body* of the base: the bottom of
  the lowest body for a supply zone, the top of the highest body for a
  demand zone.
- **Distal line.** At the extreme *wick*, per the zone formation. A reversal
  formation (rally-base-drop, or drop-base-rally for a long) uses the extreme
  wick in the **entire formation**; a continuation formation (drop-base-drop,
  or rally-base-rally for a long) uses the extreme wick in the **base or the
  leg out**.
- **Stop loss.** Standard placement: safely behind the distal line, buffered
  by a percentage of the daily ATR according to your HTF income objective —
  **10%** when the high timeframe is the weekly or greater, **2%** when it is
  the daily or less.
- **Reward-to-risk.** Every XLT setup keeps the baseline minimum of **3:1
  reward-to-risk**. Trade risk is the distance from entry to stop; divide it
  into the distance from entry to your target at the 75 – 80% target buffer
  (or use a mechanical 3:1 target). If the target zone is hit before 3:1 is
  reached, the trade is disqualified. This is a separate number from the
  profit-zone score below — the profit zone is measured in zone heights, the
  reward-to-risk against the stop.

## Scenario 1 — counter-trend reversal

**Short:** supply in the retail range, ITF uptrend (cell c).
**Long:** demand in the wholesale range, ITF downtrend (cell p).

The intermediary chart is moving against you and retail participants can see
it; the XLT case is that unfilled institutional orders remain at a fresh zone
where the trend should end.

### Profit zone — at least 3:1 to a quality zone

Verify that the target area — the nearest opposing LTF zone you judge to be
**quality** — allows a move of at least **3:1**, earning the profit-zone
enhancer at least 1 point. Because the ITF trend is running toward your entry
zone, minor pivots will likely have been built along the way. Assess those
opposing structures using your odds-enhancer criteria and verify that every
intervening structure is **weak** so there is a clear path to the 3:1 minimum
before the first quality zone can interfere. Finding the first quality zone
may require assessing several opposing zones. The app does not calculate or
enforce the target zone's quality score; that judgment belongs to the trader.

### HTF overlap

Recall that the curve is set from zones identified on the higher timeframe,
and that is where the current trend is expected to end within the overall
curve range. Identify your LTF entry zone **within** a higher-timeframe zone
— the entry zone should overlap the HTF zone. Under those circumstances,
scoring zero for trend is not much of an impact; but respect the scorecard
and wait for confirmation of a reversal before committing capital.

### Entry — confirmation only

A Scenario 1 trade is always a **confirmation entry** (7 – 8), 10¢ before
the proximal line — never a passive proximal limit. Other ways of judging a
setup's strength beyond the odds enhancers do not change the entry type.

Note the arithmetic: with the structural gates met (strength 2, freshness 2),
a Scenario 1 setup scores between 6.5 and 8. A 4-candle base (time 0.5) with
only a 3:1 profit zone lands on 6.5 — below the 7-point bar — so in practice
the setup needs a 1 – 3 candle base, a 5:1 profit zone, or both, to trade.

## Scenario 2 — aggressive trend continuation

**Short:** supply in the wholesale range, ITF downtrend (cell m).
**Long:** demand in the retail range, ITF uptrend (cell f).

The intermediary chart looks deeply in your favor — but price is at the far
end of the curve, where proximity to actual HTF opposing zones must be
checked. Shorting a minor supply rally deep in the wholesale range risks an
adverse reversal as price approaches or enters HTF demand; the mirrored long
runs the same risk into HTF supply.

### Profit zone — at least 5:1 before the first quality zone

Identify the nearest opposing zone you judge to be **quality** under your
zone-assessment criteria. The available profit zone — measured zone to zone,
as always — must reach at least **5:1**, so the profit-zone enhancer must earn
its full **2 points** to take the trade. If the first quality opposing zone
is reached before 5:1 is available, the setup is **ineligible** per the
Decision Matrix. The app does not calculate or enforce the target zone's
quality score; that judgment belongs to the trader.

### HTF proximal-line check

This is the aggressive setup's due diligence: back out to the higher
timeframe (per your income objective and duration) and verify the location of
the HTF opposing zone's **proximal line**, to ensure the 5:1 profit zone
completes before price reaches it. It is easy to fall into the habit of
ignoring HTF proximal lines, since only the distal lines set the curve.

When the HTF does not provide a clean zone formation, the curve range is set
from the most recent prominent high or low — and there is then no HTF
proximal line to check. In that case, simply locate another trading
opportunity with a valid HTF zone structure. The idea is to clearly identify
where price is likely to turn and ensure the 5:1 is available before
encountering imbalance favoring the other side.

### Entry location — proximal line at the curve extreme

The entry and the LTF zone's **proximal line must sit in the extreme third of
the curve** — the wholesale range for the short, the retail range for the
long. A zone that straddles the boundary still counts as an XLT entry as long
as the proximal line is inside the extreme range. (The app already locates
the cell from the entry proximal, so it applies this rule automatically.)

### Entry — per the scorecard

Curve scores 0 in this cell, so the ceiling is **9 out of 10**: a proximal
entry at 8.5 – 9, or a confirmation entry at 7 – 8. In practice, a Scenario 2
setup that genuinely passes every gate (strength 2, freshness 2, profit zone
2, base of 4 candles or fewer) scores 8.5 – 9 — a proximal entry. A lower
total is a sign that one of the gates was not really met.

Also verify, on arrival, that the return to your zone is not building fresh
intervening opposing zones that shrink the available profit zone below 5:1.
Judge whether any structures it builds are weak; the app does not score or
enforce the quality of target or intervening zones.

## Maximum possible score per XLT cell

Curve and trend are fixed by the matrix cell, so each XLT cell has a ceiling.
Profit zone (2), strength (2), time (1), and freshness (2) can add at most 7.

| Cell | Curve | Trend | Maximum score | Possible entries |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scenario 1 short: supply, high, uptrend (c) | 1 | 0 | **8** | Confirmation (7 – 8) only |
| Scenario 1 long: demand, low, downtrend (p) | 1 | 0 | **8** | Confirmation (7 – 8) only |
| Scenario 2 short: supply, low, downtrend (m) | 0 | 2 | **9** | Proximal (8.5 – 9) or confirmation (7 – 8) |
| Scenario 2 long: demand, high, uptrend (f) | 0 | 2 | **9** | Proximal (8.5 – 9) or confirmation (7 – 8) |

## Absolute passes

The matrix protects capital by refusing structurally hostile combinations.
Two clear examples are demand high on the curve in a downtrend and supply low
on the curve in an uptrend. Do not override a pass with a high odds-enhancer
score or a compelling-looking candle pattern, and do not override the
scorecard's entry type in a cell the matrix allows.

## XLT summary

| | Scenario 1 (reversal) | Scenario 2 (continuation) |
|---|---|---|
| Short | Supply, high on curve, uptrend (c) | Supply, low on curve, downtrend (m) |
| Long | Demand, low on curve, downtrend (p) | Demand, high on curve, uptrend (f) |
| Trend relationship | Counter-trend, anticipating reversal | With trend, at the curve extreme |
| Profit zone minimum | **3:1** to nearest quality zone (trader-assessed) | **5:1** before first quality zone (trader-assessed) |
| Intervening structures | Must score below 7 | Must score below 7 |
| HTF check | Entry zone overlaps a HTF zone | HTF proximal line beyond the 5:1; none → pass |
| Strength | 2:1+ with breakout (move out AND breakout) | Same |
| Time | ≤ 3 – 4 candles (1 – 2 optimal); 5+ disqualifies | Same |
| Freshness | Completely fresh and untested | Same |
| Max score | 8 | 9 |
| Entry (from the scorecard) | Confirmation (7 – 8) only | Proximal (8.5+) or confirmation (7 – 8) |
| Reward-to-risk baseline | ≥ 3:1 | ≥ 3:1 |

The matrix thresholds are implemented in `decisionMatrix()` in
`src/lib/trade-builder.ts`. The entry type always comes from `entryType()` on
the scorecard total. The Trade Builder enforces the scorecard thresholds and
presents only the remaining chart checks as a per-scenario checklist before
it will build the order.
